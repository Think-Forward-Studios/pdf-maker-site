Privacy Policy

PDF Maker – Edit & Sign · Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how PDF Maker – Edit & Sign (“PDF Maker” or “the app”) handles your information. The app is made by Think Forward Studio LLC (“we” or “us”). It applies to the app on iPhone and iPad and to the same app on other platforms.

The short version

  • Your documents are created, edited, read by OCR and digitally signed on your device.
  • We collect no personal data. We have no user accounts and run no servers that receive your documents.
  • No analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • A document leaves your device only if you use the optional e-sign features, and then it goes only to the e-sign server you set up yourself.
  • Extra OCR languages are downloaded from the jsDelivr network. Only the language file is requested, never your documents.

What happens on your device

Everything PDF Maker does to a document happens on your iPhone or iPad. That includes making PDFs from other files, editing pages and text, recognizing text in scans (OCR), filling in forms, adding handwritten signatures, signing with a digital ID and checking signatures. None of this sends your documents anywhere.

PDF Maker doesn’t keep a library of your documents. It reads the file you open, and when you save, it hands the finished file to the iOS share sheet so you can choose where it goes, such as Files or another app. Temporary copies the app needs while it does this stay in its own storage on your device.

When you pick a file or photo, iOS gives PDF Maker only the item you chose.

Information we collect

None. We don’t collect, receive or store personal information, documents, or usage data. You don’t need an account with us to use the app. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and it doesn’t use your device’s advertising identifier.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • Digital IDs you create or import, each kept as a password-protected .p12 file. You enter the password every time you sign with one.
  • Your saved handwritten signature and initials, if you save them.
  • OCR language data you have downloaded, so it doesn’t have to be downloaded again, and the OCR language you used last.
  • E-sign settings, if you use e-sign: the server address you entered and, while you’re signed in, your session and the name and email address of your account on that server.

This information is never sent to us, and we can’t see it. Like other app data, it may be included in your device’s iCloud or computer backup, depending on your settings. You can delete digital IDs, remove your saved signature and initials, and sign out of e-sign in the app’s Settings. Deleting the app removes all of it, so export any digital ID you want to keep first (Settings → Digital IDs → Export).

E-signature features (optional)

Sending documents for signature uses an e-sign server, which the app doesn’t include. We don’t provide or run one. To use these features you enter the address of a PDF Maker e-sign server, run by you or your organization, in Settings. Until you do, the app makes no e-sign connections.

Once a server is set, the app exchanges these with that server, and only that server:

  • the name, email address and password for your account on it, when you create an account or sign in;
  • documents you send for signature, and the details of each request, such as its title and the names and email addresses of the people you add;
  • your signature and the information you enter when you sign or decline a request through the app;
  • a PDF you check with Validate signatures, so the server can tell you whether it issued that exact document. The signatures themselves are checked on your device.

The server’s operator controls that information, and the operator’s own privacy policy applies to it. A PDF Maker e-sign server keeps an audit trail of each request, including the IP addresses, browser or device details and times of the actions taken, and adds it to the finished document. Recipients sign in their web browser through a link the server sends, and what they enter goes to the same server. We receive none of this information.

OCR language downloads

Text recognition runs entirely on your device. English is built into the app. If you choose another language, the app downloads that language’s data file once from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), a public content delivery network, and keeps it on your device for next time. When you pick such a language, the app also asks jsDelivr for the file’s size so it can show you the download size.

These requests ask only for the language file. No page images, text or other document content is sent. Like any web request, jsDelivr may see your device’s IP address and basic request information, and jsDelivr’s own privacy policy applies to that. If you only use English, the app makes no OCR downloads.

Other network use

PDF Maker connects to the internet only for the e-sign features you set up and for OCR language downloads, as described above. Everything else works offline.

Children’s privacy

PDF Maker is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. In fact, we collect no personal information from anyone.

Your choices

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You control what the app stores on your device through its Settings, or by deleting the app. For information held on an e-sign server, contact that server’s operator.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change affects how the app handles your information, we will describe it here before it takes effect in the app.
