What it does

Make PDFs from your files

Turn Word (.docx), Markdown, HTML, text, CSV, JSON and XML files, PNG and JPEG images, and other PDFs into a PDF. Pick several files to combine them into one, in order.

Edit any PDF

Add text, images, check marks, dates, drawings, highlights, boxes and white-out. Rotate, reorder, delete, extract and insert pages, and add watermarks and page numbers.

Change the text that’s there

Tap a line and retype it. The old words are removed from the file, not just covered up.

Recognize text in scans

On-device OCR makes scanned pages searchable and their lines editable; an edit erases the old words from the scan itself. English is built in, and 78 more languages download once when you need them.

Fill and build forms

Fill in forms made in Acrobat, Word and other apps, or add your own text, date, checkbox, dropdown and signature fields. Filled-in values show up in Acrobat and Preview.

Sign by hand

Draw or type your signature and initials, or use a photo of them, once. Then place them on any document.

Digital signatures

Create a digital ID or import a .p12/.pfx from a certificate authority. Sign with PAdES signatures, certify documents, and check signatures the way Acrobat does.

Request signatures

Send a document to several people to sign, in order if you like, with an audit trail and a Certificate of Completion. Needs an e-sign server address in Settings.

Private by design

Creating, editing, text recognition and digital signing all happen on your iPhone or iPad. PDF Maker has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and we don’t run any server that receives your documents. A document leaves your device only when you choose to send it for signature, and then it goes only to the e-sign server you set up.

Apart from e-sign and the first download of an extra OCR language, everything works without an internet connection. The Privacy Policy has the details.
