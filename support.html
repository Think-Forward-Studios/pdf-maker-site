Frequently asked questions

Where are my files saved?

PDF Maker doesn’t keep its own library of documents. When you tap Save, the iOS share sheet opens: choose Save to Files to keep the PDF in iCloud Drive or on your device, or send it straight to another app such as Mail. If you close the share sheet without choosing, the file isn’t saved, so save before you close the document.

How do I open a file from the Files app or another app?

In PDF Maker, tap Open & edit a PDF and choose a file from the picker. From the Files app, Mail or another app, open the file, tap the Share button and choose PDF Maker. If you don’t see it, scroll the row of apps and tap More. PDF Maker opens PDFs, Word (.docx) documents, PNG and JPEG images, and text and HTML files.

Why won’t a password-protected PDF open?

PDF Maker can’t open or edit PDFs that are locked with a password. You’ll see the message “This PDF is password-protected. Remove the password before editing it.” Remove the password in the app that added it (or with a tool such as Acrobat), then open the unlocked copy in PDF Maker.

Which languages can Recognize text (OCR) read?

English is built in and works offline. 78 more languages are available, including most European languages, Greek, Cyrillic languages such as Russian and Ukrainian, Georgian, Armenian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Choose one in the Recognize text (OCR) window; it downloads once (the window shows the size) and then stays on your device. Right-to-left scripts such as Arabic and Hebrew, Indic scripts and Thai aren’t supported yet.

How do I edit text on a scanned page?

A scanned page is a picture, so it has no text to edit until it’s recognized. Tap Edit text; on a scanned page PDF Maker says there’s no editable text and offers Recognize text. You can also choose Tools → Recognize text (OCR). Once the text has been recognized, tap a line, type your change and press Return. The old words are erased from the scan image and the new text is written in a sans-serif font.

Text is edited one line at a time. Pages are read as they appear on screen, so a sideways scan should be rotated upright first. Very large scans (over about 16 megapixels) can’t be edited on iPhone and iPad.

Request signatures asks for a server address. Why?

Sending a document for signature uses a PDF Maker e-sign server, run by you or your organization, which sends the signing links, collects the signatures and keeps the audit trail. The app doesn’t come with one, and we don’t run one. Enter its address in Settings → E-Sign server → Set server (or when the app asks), then sign in or create an account on that server. People you send documents to sign in their web browser and don’t need the app. Everything else in PDF Maker works without a server.

Why does my digital signature say “identity unknown”?

A digital ID you create in PDF Maker is self-signed: no certificate authority has vouched for it. The signature is still valid and still shows if the document changes later, but Acrobat and other apps mark the signer’s identity as unknown until the person checking it chooses to trust your certificate. That’s expected. For signatures that are trusted automatically, get a digital ID from a certificate authority and import the .p12 or .pfx file in Settings → Digital IDs → Import.

How do I keep my digital ID if I change devices?

Digital IDs and your saved signature are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. To keep a digital ID, go to Settings → Digital IDs, tap the export button next to it, and save the .p12 file somewhere safe. You can import it again on another device. Signatures you have already made stay valid either way.
